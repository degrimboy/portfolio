The story

Teen Patti has been dealt at Diwali tables for generations. Teen Patti Gold brought that table to phones, and it became one of India's top-grossing mobile card games.

I worked on what kept people at the table: VIP subscriptions, retention systems, and a web version so the game could follow players beyond their phones.

Results

  • More than 4M monthly active users
  • Around $250k monthly revenue
  • Moonfrog was acquired for $90M in December 2021

The work

Supported development of VIP subscription features for a top-grossing mobile card game.

Worked on retention systems and player experience improvements, and helped build and launch the web version with an external development team.
