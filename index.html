About

Game producer, product manager, LiveOps lead.

Senior Producer and Product Manager, running live, free-to-play games and digital products end-to-end since 2010. I lead distributed, multi-disciplinary teams — engineering, QA, art, sound, localisation — through full production pipelines, using Agile and Waterfall to keep delivery on track. My background also spans LiveOps and post-launch optimisation on high-revenue titles.

On weekends, you'll find me foraging for lost stories, old forests, and forgotten places.
