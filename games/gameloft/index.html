The story

In the early 2010s, Gameloft was putting big-screen adventures on phones: street racing in Asphalt, rooftops in Assassin's Creed, web-slinging in Spider-Man, and James Cameron's Avatar, the world's first stereoscopic 3D game.

This is where I learned to produce. I steered teams of more than fifty developers, designers and testers across continents, keeping very different games on course at the same time.

Games

The role

Led several development teams to complete top-tier mobile titles, managing more than 50 onshore and offshore developers, designers and QA testers.

Worked with offshore senior executives and business partners on design requirements, progress updates and added features.
