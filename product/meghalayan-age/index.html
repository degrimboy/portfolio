Branding and logo

Meghalaya grows organic food that deserves to be known far beyond the state. The brand had to match the care of the artisans and farmers behind it, and stand for high standards and quality.

The logo is a monogram of the company's initials, borrowing the confidence of couture fashion marks. Minimal, a little luxurious, a touch bold: simple organic products made to exacting standards.

The premium food line

Some Meghalayan produce exists nowhere else: Lakadong turmeric, with the highest curcumin content in the world, wild forest honey and white tea.

The premium range is dressed in black, with gold or silver accents. Indigenous artists drew only the outlines of the turmeric, honey and tea leaves, so the illustrations shimmer like the silver lining of a cloud.

The MAL food range

The mid-range packaging is white, with accent colours taken from the food inside each pack. With more than 30 products, white gives the range a softer footprint on the shelf while keeping the brand elegant.

Every pack carries a hand-drawn illustration of its product by indigenous artists from Meghalaya.
