The Amazing Rooster

Every FarmVille farm had its hens, its cows and its sheep. In all the years of the game, not one had ever had a rooster.

I introduced the first: the Amazing Rooster, leader of the farm animals. He wasn't handed out. He arrived in June 2016 through the Rooster Coop, a month-long Adoption Quest, and players had to raise him themselves, from egg to chick to rooster, before time ran out.

Each of the three stages leaned on a different part of the farm. Raising animals came first, then harvesting, then crafting, so the whole farm played a part in bringing him home.

Stage 1 · Collection

It starts with the hens

Players raise baby chickens five at a time, feeding each one Baby Bottles until it grows up. Some need one bottle, some need five, and the hungrier chicks pay back more.

Every chicken that reaches adulthood drops a prize, such as Speed-Grow, Fertilizer, Water or Super Feed, and lays a special egg. Nine special eggs earn a Rooster egg to hatch.

Stage 2 · Hatching

Bringing the hen to her egg

A Rooster egg needs its mother. The hen has to be coaxed across the coop to it, and the one thing that moves her is worms.

Players fill a tuning fork by harvesting crops and raising and feeding animals. When the fork is full, it charms the worms out of the ground and the hen steps closer. When she reaches the egg, it hatches.

Stage 3 · Feeding

Raising the chick

The chick is hungry, and it takes 300 Rooster Grain to raise him. Players earn it from a board of six tasks: watering, fertilizing and harvesting crops, crafting items, and asking friends for help.

This was the longest stretch of the quest. Some tasks asked for 98 crafts or a couple of hundred crops, and seeing them through was what turned a chick into a leader.

The reward

The leader of the farm

When the last of the grain is in, the chick grows into the Amazing Rooster and takes his place in the coop. Then he gets to work.

  • He raises the farm's water capacity by 10, from 30 to 40, for 28 days
  • He waters your trees and groves
  • When his watering uses run out, one click has him feed every adult chicken on your main farm, once every 24 hours
  • Players who wanted him to stay longer could extend his visit for 15 Farmbucks

The launch

Zynga announced him with a trailer, "Get the Amazing Rooster!" Before long, players were filming their own hunts for Rooster Eggs.
