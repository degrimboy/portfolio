Role
Associate Game Producer & Designer

Results

  • Top 10 overall grossing app on iTunes in 2013 and 2014
  • Around 4M monthly active users, 1M daily, and more than $1M monthly revenue
  • Feature design that lifted the monthly revenue baseline by 30%
  • Quarterly Performance Award, June 2014
  • Part of the growth phase that led to GSN's $165M acquisition in 2014

Production

Ran content production for the game and managed sales and campaigns across Facebook, Android and iOS, along with in-game copy and the social media community.

Planned and delivered the product roadmap on a regular deployment cycle, shipping retention and LiveOps features.

Design

Conceived and maintained the game design documents for Bingo Bash's trademarked mechanics.

Wrote specs, including UI and UX design, for cross-platform features aimed at retention and acquisition.
