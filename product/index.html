SaaS · Procurement & accounts payable

Dentira

A dental group can run hundreds of clinics, each ordering supplies from dozens of vendors, and every delivery brings an invoice. Dentira brings the buying and the paying into one place, and some of the largest dental groups use it to run their procurement.

I own live product delivery: the roadmap, sprint planning and releases, working with design and engineering on how the workflows behave.

Much of my work sits in the invoice lifecycle: approvals, sending invoices back for correction, bulk actions, role-based permissions, and syncing to accounting systems. I started with a full UX audit and turned it into personas, interactive mockups and a phased improvement plan.
